# Skill: Content System

## Trigger

Use for CodeToday, Personal, Quant Base, editorial planning, content pipelines, social derivatives, publishing states, or content analytics.

## Goal

Make one strong idea reusable across channels without turning every platform post into separate planning overhead.

## Domain model direction

A **master content item** represents the core idea. Platform-specific assets are derivatives.

Example:

`Master idea → YouTube video → Shorts/Reels/TikTok clips → LinkedIn/Instagram derivatives`

## Brand boundaries

### CodeToday

Programming, building, learning, and action. English-first identity. Brand philosophy: `Why not today?`

### Personal

Technology, science, career, ideas, motivation, and personal topics.

### Quant Base

Institutional/professional authority, education, product/project building, and industry-relevant content.

Do not automatically cross-post every piece across every brand.

## Pipeline

Preferred lifecycle:

`Idea → Research → Script → Record → Edit → Thumbnail → Schedule → Published → Analyze`

Stages may be simplified for formats that do not require every step.

## Workflow

1. Capture the core idea with minimal friction.
2. Assign brand/topic only when useful; Inbox may stay unorganized initially.
3. Define the master content objective and audience promise.
4. Create platform derivatives from the master item.
5. Track production stage, not just publish date.
6. After publication, attach useful performance observations to improve future decisions.

## Guardrails

- Do not create duplicate master items merely because the same idea appears on multiple platforms.
- Keep platform metadata separate from the core idea.
- Do not make daily posting volume the product goal.
- The system should support sustainable production, not pressure the owner into feeding every network independently.

## Definition of done

- [ ] brand is explicit where necessary;
- [ ] master idea and derivatives are distinguishable;
- [ ] production stage is clear;
- [ ] one idea can produce multiple deliverables without duplication;
- [ ] workflow remains easy to use from mobile.
